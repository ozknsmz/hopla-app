HOPLA.

Hold. Release. Hop. A calm, one-touch jumping game for iPhone.

The game

Press and hold to charge, release to jump to the next object. The longer you hold, the farther you go. Land in the center for a perfect hop, chain perfect hops for bigger combos, and see how far you can get.

  • Endless course that slowly gets harder, with calm breaks every 100 steps.
  • Travel through different worlds: Room, Garden, Harbor and Winter.
  • Collect coins and unlock new characters.
  • Climb the global leaderboard. No sign-up needed.
