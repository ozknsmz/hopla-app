Privacy Policy

Hopla: Jump & Hop (iOS) · Effective date: 29 October 2026

This policy explains what data the Hopla mobile game ("Hopla", "the app") collects, why, who receives it and what choices you have. Hopla is designed to collect as little as possible: there is no sign-up, and we never ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

1. Who is responsible

Hopla is developed and published by Özkan Semiz, an individual developer ("we", "us"), who is the data controller (in Turkish law: veri sorumlusu) for the data described below.
Contact: smzozkn@gmail.com

2. Data we collect

2.1 Online game account (leaderboard)

On first launch the app silently creates an anonymous account with our backend provider Supabase. It is not connected to your Apple ID, e-mail or real name. We store:

DataDetails
Random user IDA random identifier (UUID) generated for your anonymous account, plus a session token kept on your device so you stay the same player.
NicknameAutomatically generated (for example "Player1234" or "Oyuncu1234"). You can change it. Your nickname is public: other players see it on the leaderboard. Please do not use your real name or personal information as a nickname.
ScoresYour best score, the number of steps in that run, and timestamps (when your account was created and when you set your best score). Your rank is calculated from these.
Reports and blocksIf you report a nickname, we store your user ID, the reported player's user ID and nickname, the reason and the time. If you block a player, the list of players you blocked is stored so that their nickname is hidden for you.
Technical request dataWhen the app talks to our server, your device's IP address and basic request information are necessarily processed to deliver the response and to protect the service against abuse. We do not store IP addresses in our database; our hosting provider may keep them in short-lived security and access logs.

2.2 Data that stays on your device

Your coins, unlocked characters, settings (sound, vibration, language), daily gift progress and local records are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data; we cannot restore it.

2.3 Advertising (Google AdMob)

Hopla offers optional rewarded ads (for example: watch an ad to continue a run, get bonus coins or double your coins). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, Google's software in the app collects data such as your IP address (from which an approximate location may be derived), device and operating-system information, an app-scoped device identifier (IDFV), ad interaction data and diagnostic data.

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA): collected only if you allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not available to the app or Google. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): before ads are personalised, the app shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform). You can choose personalised or non-personalised ads, or refuse consent. You can reopen this choice later from the app's Settings (Privacy / ad choices) where available.
  • If you do not consent to personalised advertising, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads, which use contextual information and still require some data for fraud prevention, frequency capping and measurement.

We do not receive your IDFA or ad data in a form that identifies you; we only receive aggregated reports from Google. Google processes this data under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

2.4 In-app purchases (Apple)

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment card details, Apple ID, name or billing address. The app only receives a confirmation from Apple that a product was purchased (and, for "Restore Purchases", which non-consumable products you own) so that it can deliver the item. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

2.5 What we do not collect

No contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location, health data, e-mail address or phone number. There is no chat and no free-form messaging between players. We do not sell your personal data.

4. Who receives the data

  • Supabase (Supabase Inc.) – hosts our database and anonymous authentication. Data is stored in the European Union (Ireland, AWS eu-west-1). Supabase acts as our processor.
  • Google (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC) – AdMob advertising and the User Messaging Platform consent tool (see 2.3).
  • Apple – App Store distribution and in-app purchase processing.
  • Other players – see your nickname, score, steps and rank on the leaderboard.
  • Authorities – only if we are legally required to.

International transfers. Our database is in the EU. Google and Apple may process data in the United States and other countries; they rely on safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. For users in Turkey, transfers abroad take place under KVKK Article 9 (for example standard contracts notified to the Personal Data Protection Authority, or your consent where required).

5. How long we keep data

  • Account, nickname and scores: as long as your anonymous account exists. Accounts with no activity for 24 months may be deleted automatically.
  • Reports: up to 12 months after the report is resolved, unless needed longer to deal with repeated abuse.
  • Block list: until you unblock the player or delete your data.
  • Server logs: kept by our hosting provider for a short period (typically days) for security and debugging.
  • Backups: deleted data may remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before being overwritten.
  • Advertising data: retained by Google according to its own policies.

6. Deleting your data

  • In the app: open Settings → Delete my data and confirm. This permanently deletes your anonymous account, nickname, scores, reports you made and your block list from our server. Coins, characters and your best score stored only on your device are kept. It cannot be undone.
  • By e-mail: write to smzozkn@gmail.com with your nickname as shown on the leaderboard (and, if possible, a screenshot of your Settings or leaderboard screen) so we can find your anonymous account. We respond within 30 days.
  • Uninstalling the app removes local data, but does not delete your leaderboard entry on our server. Use one of the options above.
  • Purchases are recorded by Apple, not by us; deleting your data does not cancel or refund purchases. Non-consumable purchases can be restored with Settings → Restore Purchases.

7. Your rights

GDPR (EEA/UK users): you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object to processing of your personal data, the right to data portability, and the right to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting earlier processing). You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

KVKK (Turkey) – Law No. 6698, Article 11: you have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed; to request information about it; to learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly; to know the third parties to whom it is transferred in Turkey or abroad; to request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data; to request deletion or destruction under Article 7; to request that third parties be notified of such correction or deletion; to object to a result against you arising exclusively from automated analysis; and to claim compensation for damages arising from unlawful processing. You may also complain to the Personal Data Protection Board (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu).

Other regions (for example California): we do not sell or share personal data for money. Where local law gives you similar rights, contact us and we will honour them.

To exercise any right, e-mail smzozkn@gmail.com. Because accounts are anonymous, we may ask for your nickname or a screenshot to find your data. We reply within 30 days (KVKK: within 30 days at the latest, free of charge).

8. Children

Hopla is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data (for example a real name as a nickname), contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

All communication between the app and our server uses encrypted HTTPS (TLS). Players cannot read the database directly; the app can only call a small set of server functions. Access to the backend is restricted to the developer.

10. Changes

We may update this policy, for example when the app gets new features. The effective date at the top will change, and significant changes will be announced in the app or on this page.
